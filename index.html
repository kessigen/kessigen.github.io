SAC Baselines for Robotic Pick-and-Place

Apr 2026

Compared reinforcement learning methods on a simulated WidowX-250 arm. I built the MuJoCo pick-and-place environment, including the reward shaping that finally stopped the arm hovering next to the cube, and the SAC baselines the contrastive RL models were measured against: SAC on pose observations reached 90% success, and SAC + DrQ on camera images reached 50%. For the image baseline I tried SB3's CNN policy, a custom pixel encoder and a SAC autoencoder before settling on DrQ. Against these baselines, the project's contrastive RL method, built by my teammate, raised image-based success from 50% to 80%, and a 32-layer version reached 85%.
